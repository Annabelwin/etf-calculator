Why some investors choose DGRO

Income can grow over time

Companies that increase dividends can compound your income stream.

Focus on quality growth

Dividend growth often reflects stronger underlying businesses.

Lower reliance on yield

Less dependent on current high payouts, more on future increases.

Fits long-term compounding

Works best when held over many years, not for immediate income.

When DGRO fits — and when it may frustrate you

You value long-term income growth

  • You can wait for income to increase over time
  • You focus on compounding, not immediate cash flow
  • You accept lower starting yield

You may prefer SCHD or VYM if

  • You want higher income now
  • You rely on dividends as cash flow
  • You prefer simpler, more predictable payouts
Core insight
