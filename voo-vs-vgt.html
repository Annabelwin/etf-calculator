VOO vs VGT

Broad Market vs Technology ETF

VOO and VGT represent two different ways to invest in the U.S. stock market. VOO tracks the S&P 500 and offers broad exposure to large U.S. companies across many industries. VGT focuses specifically on the technology sector and holds companies from the information technology industry.

In simple terms: VOO provides diversified exposure to the U.S. market, while VGT focuses heavily on the technology sector.

VOO vs VGT: main differences

Diversification

VOO includes companies from many sectors including healthcare, finance, consumer goods, and technology. VGT focuses only on technology companies, which makes it more concentrated.

Growth potential

Technology companies have historically shown strong growth. Because of this, VGT may outperform during strong technology-driven markets.

Risk and volatility

Because VGT is concentrated in one sector, it may experience larger price swings than VOO.

Investment role

VOO is often used as a core portfolio holding. VGT is typically used as a sector tilt for investors who want additional exposure to technology companies.

Is VGT better than VOO?

VGT is not necessarily better than VOO. It simply focuses on a specific sector. Investors who want a broad diversified portfolio often prefer VOO, while those seeking higher technology exposure may add VGT.

Can investors hold both?

Yes. Some investors use VOO as a core holding and add VGT to increase exposure to the technology sector.

VOO vs VGT FAQ

Is VGT more aggressive than VOO?

Yes. VGT focuses on the technology sector and may experience higher volatility.

Is VOO more diversified?

Yes. VOO includes companies from multiple sectors across the S&P 500.

Can I hold both ETFs?

Yes. Many investors combine VOO with sector ETFs like VGT.
