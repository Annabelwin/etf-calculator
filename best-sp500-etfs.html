What matters more

Returns matter — but behavior matters more

The best ETF can still fail you

If you cannot stay invested during volatility, returns don’t matter.

The wrong ETF is often a behavior mismatch

Small structural differences matter less than your ability to hold.

Simple often beats “optimal”

The more complex your decision, the harder it is to stay consistent.

Clarity creates staying power

A clear default reduces hesitation and helps you actually invest.

Decision

How to choose between VOO, IVV, and SPY

• Choose VOO if you want the simplest long-term default
• Choose IVV if you want a nearly identical alternative
• Choose SPY only if liquidity or trading matters to you

This is not a performance decision — it is a structure and usage decision.
