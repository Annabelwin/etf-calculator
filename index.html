Core philosophy

Our investment principles

This platform is built for long-term investing ideas: clearer decisions, lower costs, resilience under uncertainty, and respect for market cycles.

Munger

Avoid obvious mistakes. Better investing often begins with better decisions, not more complexity.

Bogle

Keep costs low, diversify broadly, and stay with a plan that is strong enough to hold.

Taleb

A fragile strategy depends on prediction. A stronger one can survive uncertainty and error.

Marks

Risk changes across moods and cycles. Better investors respect that context instead of ignoring it.
