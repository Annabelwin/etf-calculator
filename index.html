Decision principles

Our investment principles.

The platform is built around enduring ideas that help investors make clearer choices and avoid fragile decisions.

Munger

Avoid obvious mistakes. A better process often matters more than a clever prediction.

 Bogle

Keep costs low, diversify broadly, and make the plan simple enough to stick with.

 Taleb

Protect against fragility. Survival comes first. Upside should not depend on precision.

 Marks

Markets move in cycles. Behavior and discipline often matter more than forecasts.
