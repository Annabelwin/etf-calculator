Our investment principles

This platform is built on four ideas: clearer decisions, lower costs, resilience under uncertainty, and respect for market cycles.

Munger

Avoid obvious mistakes. Better investing often begins with better decisions, not more complexity.

Bogle

Keep costs low, diversify broadly, and stay with a plan simple enough to keep following.

Taleb

A fragile strategy depends on prediction. A stronger one can survive uncertainty and continue.

Marks

Risk changes across moods and cycles. Better investors respect that context instead of ignoring it.

These principles are not decoration. They explain why this platform is designed the way it is.
