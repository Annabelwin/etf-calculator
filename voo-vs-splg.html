VOO vs SPLG

VOO and SPLG are both low-cost ETFs that track the S&P 500. Because they follow the same index, many investors compare them when choosing a simple long-term core holding.

Key Differences

VOO and SPLG are extremely similar because both track the same S&P 500 index. The main differences are fund provider, expense ratio, and size. VOO is issued by Vanguard and is one of the most popular ETFs in the world. SPLG is issued by State Street and often has a slightly lower expense ratio. For many long-term investors, the differences are small enough that either ETF may work well as a core position.

Which ETF Is Better?

VOO may be more attractive for investors who prefer Vanguard and want one of the largest and most widely used S&P 500 ETFs. SPLG may appeal to investors who want a very low-cost option and are comfortable using a smaller, though still highly established, fund. For most long-term investors, consistency matters more than the small differences between these two ETFs.
