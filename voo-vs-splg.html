VOO vs SPLG

What Actually Matters for Long-Term Investors

Since both ETFs track the same S&P 500 index, their holdings and long-term performance are usually extremely similar.

The difference most investors notice first is the expense ratio. SPLG is slightly cheaper than VOO, although the difference is very small for long-term investors.

VOO, however, is one of the most widely held ETFs in the world. Many investors prefer it simply because it is familiar, highly liquid, and widely trusted.

For long-term investing, the real decision usually comes down to personal preference rather than a major structural difference.

Which One Fits Your Investing Style?

If you want one of the most widely used S&P 500 ETFs with very strong liquidity and recognition, VOO is often the easier choice.

If your goal is minimizing expense ratios as much as possible, SPLG may appeal because of its slightly lower cost.

In practice, both funds provide nearly identical exposure to the S&P 500, which means either ETF can work well as a long-term core holding.
