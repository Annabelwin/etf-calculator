VOO Calculator FAQ

What is VOO?

VOO is the Vanguard S&P 500 ETF. It tracks the S&P 500 index and gives investors exposure to many of the largest companies in the United States.

What is the average return of VOO?

Historically, the S&P 500 has returned around 8% to 10% annually over the long term, though actual returns vary from year to year.

Is VOO a good long-term investment?

Many investors consider VOO a strong long-term investment because it is diversified, simple, and widely used for passive investing.

How does this VOO calculator work?

This calculator estimates how your investment could grow using compound returns and regular monthly contributions over time.
