SCHD vs VYM

SCHD and VYM are two of the most popular dividend ETFs for long-term investors. Both focus on companies that pay consistent dividends, but they use different strategies and selection methods. Investors often compare them to understand which dividend approach better fits their portfolio.

What Actually Matters for Dividend Investors

Although SCHD and VYM both focus on dividend-paying companies, their strategies are slightly different.

SCHD selects companies based on dividend growth and financial quality metrics. It tends to concentrate more on firms with strong balance sheets and consistent dividend increases.

VYM focuses more broadly on companies with relatively high dividend yields across the U.S. stock market. As a result, it holds more companies and is generally more diversified.

For long-term investors, the difference often comes down to strategy: dividend growth versus high dividend yield.

Which One Fits Your Investing Style?

Investors who prefer companies with strong dividend growth and quality screening may prefer SCHD.

Those who want broad exposure to high-dividend stocks across many sectors may prefer VYM.

Both funds can work well as long-term income-focused investments depending on portfolio goals.

SCHD vs VYM FAQ

Do SCHD and VYM invest in the same companies?
They overlap somewhat, but SCHD holds fewer stocks and focuses more on dividend growth quality.

Is SCHD better than VYM?
Neither is universally better. SCHD emphasizes dividend growth and quality, while VYM focuses on higher dividend yield.

Which ETF has higher diversification?
VYM typically holds more companies and therefore offers broader diversification.

Can investors hold both?
Yes. Some investors combine both strategies to balance dividend growth and yield.
