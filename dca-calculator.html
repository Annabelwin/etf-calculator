Scenario Thinking

Markets are unpredictable. Instead of relying on a single outcome, consider how your plan performs across different return scenarios.

Why DCA Works

Dollar-cost averaging spreads your investments over time, reducing the risk of investing at the wrong moment. It turns volatility into an advantage by buying more when prices are lower.

Decision Guide

  • Are you investing long-term (10+ years)?
  • Can you stay consistent regardless of market conditions?
  • Do you prefer discipline over trying to predict markets?

A Common Mistake

Many investors stop investing during downturns. This often leads to worse long-term outcomes than simply staying consistent.
