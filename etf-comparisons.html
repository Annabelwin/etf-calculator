How to Use This Page

Most ETF comparison questions are really about one of four things: cost, diversification, growth potential, or income.

That is why the comparisons on this site are grouped by investor goal rather than just listing random ETF names. The idea is simple: help you find the decision you are actually trying to make.

Why Investors Compare ETFs

Investors usually compare ETFs because they want to simplify a decision: broad market or focused exposure, growth or income, lower cost or higher liquidity.

That is why the pages on this site focus on practical investor questions instead of just listing fund facts. The point is not only to compare numbers, but to help you understand which ETF fits the way you actually invest.
