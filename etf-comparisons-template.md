# ETF Comparison Page Template

This document defines the standard structure for all ETF comparison pages on this site.

All comparison pages should follow the same layout to maintain design consistency and SEO structure.

## 1 Hero Section

Purpose: Explain what this comparison is about in 2–3 sentences.

Structure:

H1  
Subtitle  
1 short paragraph

Example:

VOO vs SPY

VOO and SPY both track the S&P 500, so at first glance they look almost identical.  
But investors still compare them because cost, trading style and long-term use cases are not exactly the same.

## 3 What Actually Matters

Purpose: Explain the real difference between the ETFs.

Structure:

H2  
3–4 paragraphs

Typical logic:

* Why investors compare them  
* Key structural difference  
* Long-term investing perspective  
* Trading / liquidity differences

---

## 4 Which One Fits Your Investing Style

Purpose: Help the reader decide.

Structure:

H2  
2 short paragraphs  
1 concluding sentence

Example logic:

Long-term investors → lower cost ETF  
Active traders → higher liquidity ETF

End with:

"The most important thing is choosing an ETF you can hold through market cycles."

* Why do investors compare them?
