VOO vs VYM

VOO and VYM are two popular Vanguard ETFs. VOO tracks the S&P 500 while VYM focuses on high dividend stocks.

ETF Overview

ETF VOO VYM
Full Name Vanguard S&P 500 ETF Vanguard High Dividend Yield ETF
Expense Ratio 0.03% 0.06%
Dividend Yield ~1.4% ~3%
Focus Large-cap US companies High dividend US companies

Performance Differences

Historically, VOO tends to outperform high dividend ETFs like VYM over very long periods because growth companies reinvest earnings rather than distributing them as dividends. However, VYM can provide more stable income and may be attractive to investors who prefer higher dividend payouts.

Which ETF Is Better?

VOO may be better for long-term growth investors who want broad exposure to the S&P 500. VYM may be better for investors seeking dividend income and lower volatility. Many investors hold both ETFs for diversification.
