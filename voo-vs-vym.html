VOO vs VYM

VOO and VYM are two popular Vanguard ETFs. VOO focuses on the S&P 500 while VYM targets companies with higher dividend yields.

ETF Overview

ETF VOO VYM
Full Name Vanguard S&P 500 ETF Vanguard High Dividend Yield ETF
Expense Ratio 0.03% 0.06%
Dividend Yield ~1.4% ~3%
Strategy Large US companies High dividend companies

Performance Differences

VOO tracks the S&P 500 index and therefore represents the performance of large-cap US companies. VYM selects stocks with above-average dividend yields and therefore tends to include more value-oriented companies. Historically, growth-focused funds like VOO may outperform dividend-focused funds during strong economic expansion.

Which ETF Is Better?

VOO may be better for investors seeking long-term capital growth and broad exposure to the US stock market. VYM may be more attractive for investors who want higher dividend income and potentially lower volatility. Some investors combine both ETFs to balance growth and income.
