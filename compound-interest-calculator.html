Compound Interest Calculator FAQ

What is compound interest?

Compound interest means your returns earn additional returns over time, helping growth accelerate as the years pass.

How does this calculator work?

It estimates future value using your starting amount, monthly contributions, time horizon, and annual return assumption.

Why are monthly contributions important?

Regular contributions increase your invested capital and can significantly improve long-term results through consistency.

Why is time so powerful in compounding?

The longer your money stays invested, the more opportunities it has to generate returns on previous gains.
