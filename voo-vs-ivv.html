Quick Decision

VOO vs IVV: quick answer

Choose VOO
VOO is widely used by long-term investors who prefer Vanguard’s structure and ecosystem.
Choose IVV
IVV is often chosen for institutional use and slightly different fund structure advantages.
For most long-term investors, this is not a meaningful decision — choosing either VOO or IVV will likely lead to the same outcome.

What Matters

What actually matters for investors

  • Both ETFs track the same index with nearly identical cost.
  • Long-term performance differences are negligible.
  • Your behavior matters far more than choosing between these two funds.
Decision Fit

Which one fits you?

  • VOO fits you if you prefer Vanguard and a simple long-term approach.
  • IVV fits you if you are already using BlackRock products or platforms.
  • Either works — the difference is unlikely to affect your long-term outcome.
Reality Check

The difference is almost negligible

VOO and IVV are essentially two wrappers around the same idea. Over long periods, your results will depend far more on how long you stay invested than which one you choose.

If you are investing for decades, choosing between VOO and IVV is a second-order decision. Staying invested is the first-order decision.

Limitations

What neither ETF solves

  • No international diversification.
  • No protection from market declines.
  • Not suitable for short-term needs.
Suggested Strategy

What a rational investor might do

  • Pick either VOO or IVV and stay consistent.
  • Do not switch between them based on small differences.
  • The biggest risk is abandoning your plan, not choosing the wrong ETF.
