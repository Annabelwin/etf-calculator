VOO vs IVV

Which S&P 500 ETF may be better for long-term investors?

VOO and IVV are two of the most popular ETFs for investors who want simple, low-cost exposure to the S&P 500. Because both funds track the same index, their performance is usually very close. That is why many investors compare them when choosing a long-term core holding.

In simple terms: VOO and IVV are extremely similar. For most long-term investors, the decision often comes down to fund provider preference, structure, and small differences in cost or convenience.

What do VOO and IVV have in common?

Both VOO and IVV track the S&P 500 Index. That means both funds give investors exposure to around 500 of the largest publicly traded companies in the United States. They are both widely used as core long-term ETFs for retirement accounts and buy-and-hold investing.

VOO vs IVV: main differences

Expense ratio

VOO and IVV are both known for very low fees. For many long-term investors, the difference in expense ratio is so small that it may not be the deciding factor.

Fund provider

VOO is issued by Vanguard, while IVV is issued by iShares. Some investors prefer one provider over the other based on brand trust, brokerage convenience, or existing portfolio setup.

Structure and size

Both ETFs are large, liquid, and established. In practice, both are considered highly efficient S&P 500 ETFs for long-term investors.

Long-term investing fit

Because the two funds are so similar, many long-term investors simply choose whichever fits better with their brokerage, automatic investment plan, or personal preference.

Is IVV better than VOO?

IVV is not necessarily better than VOO. For most long-term investors, the two funds are close enough that the difference is likely to be minor. The more important factor is staying consistent and continuing to invest over time.

Is VOO more popular than IVV?

VOO is often more widely discussed among retail investors, while IVV is also very large and heavily used. Both funds are respected, well-known, and commonly used for long-term S&P 500 exposure.

Which one should beginners choose?

Many beginners can choose either VOO or IVV and still get nearly identical market exposure. The better choice is often the one that feels simpler to hold consistently for many years.
