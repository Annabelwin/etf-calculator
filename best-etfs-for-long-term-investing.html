Core idea

The best long-term ETF is the one you can keep holding

Long-term investing is not about picking the perfect ETF. It is about choosing a structure you can survive — and stick with — for years.

For most long-term investors, a simple, low-cost, broad ETF is the strongest starting point.

What most people miss

Returns matter — but behavior matters more

The best ETF can fail if you cannot hold it

High returns are meaningless if volatility forces you to sell at the wrong time.

The wrong ETF is often a behavior mismatch

Most mistakes come from choosing something you cannot stick with.

Simple beats optimal in the long run

A slightly less “optimal” ETF that you hold for 20 years beats a perfect one you abandon.

Clarity creates consistency

A clear path removes hesitation and helps you stay invested through cycles.
